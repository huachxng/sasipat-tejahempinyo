I founded MEEKVEGGIES, a freeze-dried fruit, vegetable, and mushroom brand for the Thai market, and began the brand identity and market research in February 2026. In June 2026 I called the D'Nuts factory, negotiated a reseller arrangement in which the factory packs and ships each order directly to the customer for a flat ฿50, and confirmed the deal on 17 June 2026. The market research, margin model, and launch plan were drafted with an AI assistant and tested against the factory's terms. The brand launched in 2026 and sells on Shopee, LINE and Facebook, with the first customers among my mother's employees, who had tested the samples.
