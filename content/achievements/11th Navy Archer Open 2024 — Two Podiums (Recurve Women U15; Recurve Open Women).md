At the 11th Navy Archer Open, organized by the Royal Thai Navy Archery Committee at Ban Chang, Rayong, from 22 to 24 November 2024, I placed 1st runner-up in the Recurve Women U15 ranking round and 2nd runner-up in the Recurve Open Women elimination round. Both certificates are in my portfolio.
