DAEMON is a rules-based trading daemon I designed for Thai-listed depositary receipts on US stocks. It ranks a universe of US underlyings by momentum, converts the picks into order tickets on the matching Thai-listed receipts, enforces protective exits and a hard capital floor, and publishes a bilingual Thai/English ledger. It never touches an account: a human places every order at the broker. It ran as a two-month live experiment on the Stock Exchange of Thailand from 15 August 2026. The code was written with an AI assistant, and the repository and ledger are public.
