At the 23rd Kasetsart Open Archery Competition, held at Kasetsart University's Bangkhen campus with the National Archery Association of Thailand from 13 to 16 March 2025, I placed 1st runner-up in the Recurve Women Under-25 70 m event. I scored 560 points over 72 arrows. Shooting up an age group against under-25 archers was a step up from the youth categories I had competed in until then.
