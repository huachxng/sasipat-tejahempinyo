Career Pathway: Your Future Guide is an interview series I started to give students something more useful than a vague job description. Each episode takes one career and walks through it in order: what the work actually is, the skills and classes to build now, realistic salaries, how to choose a university and a major, and the habits that separate the people who make it. Guests are teachers, counsellors and professionals from our community. I host, record, cut and grade the episodes myself, and any profits are pledged to charity.

**EP1 — High School Path to Quant** (17 min). What a quant does, what the job pays, the classes to take in high school, the competitions and portfolio work that count, and the transcript advice nobody tells you. Recorded with two of our teachers. Three short reels came out of it: starting salaries, high-school classes, and "what exactly is a quant".

**EP2 — High School Path to Low-Level Systems (Computer Engineer)** (25 min). With CS and STEM teacher Martin Shores and student builder Phanuwich "Jiaxin" Arayadacho: what low-level engineering is, why math comes first, CS versus computer engineering versus EE versus data science, why hardware engineers are hard to replace with AI, and Jiaxin's bare-metal bootloader project. Cut from 4K masters with chapter markers, captions and four shorts.
