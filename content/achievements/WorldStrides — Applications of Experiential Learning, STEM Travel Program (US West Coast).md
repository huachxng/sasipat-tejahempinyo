In summer 2023 I completed WorldStrides' three-week STEM educational travel program, Applications of Experiential Learning, on the US West Coast from 25 June to 16 July. The itinerary included UC Berkeley, Stanford, the Monterey Bay Aquarium, Boeing's Future of Flight, Crater Lake, and a BASIS Independent school. The certificate is dated 16 July 2023.
