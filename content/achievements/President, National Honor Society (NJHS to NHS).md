I was inducted into the National Junior Honor Society on 28 April 2023 and into the National Honor Society on 17 January 2025, and have led the BISB chapter as president for two years from September 2023. Over that time the chapter ran service projects at school level, with the TUSKERS community, and with outside NGOs, and raised more than 40,000 THB for charity. Our aim was to turn the school's attention outward, from projects inside the fence to the community around it.
