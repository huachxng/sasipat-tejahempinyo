MEEKVEGGIES is a small freeze-dried produce brand I founded in 2026 to sell on Shopee and Lazada. On the factory call I introduced myself as Noah, the name I write under here.

## The factory deal

On 12 June 2026 I called D'Nuts, a Thai manufacturer that sells under the name "Natural Healthy", to ask for a reseller arrangement in which the factory handled packaging and, ideally, shipping. The factory confirmed it would dropship each order for a flat 50 THB fee and let me mix products within one order. No inventory, no storage, almost no cash at risk before a sale: startup cost fell from most of a 5,000 THB budget to little more than a 500-1,000 THB sample set.

D'Nuts makes two lines: standard nuts and dried fruit, and a premium "crispy" freeze-dried fruit, vegetable and mushroom line that includes okra, shiitake, mixed vegetables, mangosteen and longan. The range runs to about 17 products.

## The premium pivot

The original plan was a cheap reseller shop. The deal changed that. Wel-B and Greenday own the small-pack shelf in Thailand, and the factory sells direct at its own retail prices, so undercutting was never an option. The strategy became a premium brand sold at or above factory retail, with margin coming from a reseller cost well below it. Vegetable and mushroom crisps became the differentiator; the planned hero products were crispy mixed veg, shiitake, strawberry, mango and a five-pack gift box.

The gate for the whole business is one number: reseller cost at or below about 40% of my selling price, never above about 55%. Modelled at 50% of factory retail with an 8% platform fee, the average margin came out near 54%, and a 1,000 THB sample spend breaks even in roughly 8 to 12 orders.

## What I learned

Know the one number that decides everything before the call, and do not reveal your budget. Get prices and the Thai FDA registration number in writing; no registration, no listing. A good deal can change your positioning entirely, so build the feasibility model to recalculate the moment real numbers arrive. Unit economics of this kind is the most concrete microeconomics I have done, and it feeds [[Teaching Economics]]. The instinct to make something opaque legible also drove [[Quant Career Pathway]], and the "decide the criterion before you see the result" habit runs through [[AI Bubble Research]].
