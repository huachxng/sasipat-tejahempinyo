I shoot recurve for Thailand's youth national team. In August 2025 I represented Thailand at the [[World Archery Youth Championships 2025]] in Canada, and I co-lead [[Devil Maker Archery]] with my coach.

## How recurve works

A recurve bow's limbs curve away from the archer at the tips, which stores more energy for a given draw length than a straight-limbed bow. The Olympic form of the discipline adds a sight, a clicker that drops off the arrow point at a fixed draw length, and stabilisers, but no cams or let-off: the archer holds the full draw weight at anchor and releases with the fingers. Outdoor recurve at senior level is shot at 70 metres. Consistency comes from repeating the same draw length, anchor and release, which is what the clicker exists to enforce.

## What training taught me

A scoring end is a sequence of identical shots, and identical shots come from a process you can repeat when you are tired or behind. That transfers directly. When I wrote the success criteria for the [[Bubble Intensity Score]] before running the backtest, it was the same idea: decide what a good shot looks like before you see where the arrow lands. Spending about a month off equipment in the summer of 2026 while I was at Stanford was a reminder of how quickly form drifts without repetition.
