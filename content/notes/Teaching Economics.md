## Why teach it

The paper's motivation is financial literacy: experts have econometric tests, ordinary investors have headlines. Lusardi and Mitchell (2014) provide the evidence that most people cannot read financial conditions unaided. A senior assisting in AP Micro is a small version of the same problem. If I can explain why a composite of four z-scores is more useful than CAPE alone to a classmate who has just met elasticity, the [[Bubble Intensity Score]] is doing what it was designed to do.

The teaching also runs the other way. Explaining margin, platform fees and break-even to a class is easier when you have built a pricing model for a real product, which is what [[MEEKVEGGIES]] required. And the reason I made [[Quant Career Pathway]] was that the careers side of economics was even less legible to students than the theory.
